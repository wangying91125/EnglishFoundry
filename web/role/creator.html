📖 正文 · CEFR B1

European regulators fine a social network 430 million euros over data transfers

Regulators in the European Union have fined a large social network 430 million euros. The company sent personal data of European users to servers in the United States. Under EU law, such transfers need strong legal protection, and officials found that protection was missing. The network serves about thirty million users in Europe, so the case affects many people. Officials ordered the company to stop the transfers within six months. A company spokesperson said it disagrees with the ruling and will appeal, so the final penalty may still change.

📚 核心词汇（8 个）

#单词词性中文英文释义例句
1regulatorn.监管机构；监管者An official body or person that makes sure companies obey the rules of an industry.The Irish regulator opened a new inquiry into how the app handles children's data.
2fine ⭐v.罚款To make a person or company pay money as a punishment for breaking a rule.Authorities fined the operator after discovering that safety checks had been skipped.
3transfern.传输；转移（数据）The movement of something from one place, system or country to another.Every international money transfer is recorded and checked for fraud.
4personal data ⭐n. phr.个人数据；个人信息Information about a person that can identify them, such as a name, address or phone number.Giving your date of birth means handing over personal data to a stranger.
5safeguardn.保障措施A measure designed to prevent harm or misuse.Encryption is a basic safeguard for any company that stores customer records.
6violatev.违反；侵犯To break a law, rule or agreement.The court found that the site had violated users' privacy rights.
7rulingn.裁决；判决An official decision made by a court or a body with authority.The ruling surprised many analysts, who had expected a much smaller penalty.
8appeal ⭐v.上诉；申诉To ask a higher court or authority to examine and change a decision.The firm said it will appeal the decision within the next three months.

🎧 听力稿（B1 口语版）

Hi, welcome back to today's easy news. Let's talk about a big fine. Regulators in the European Union have just told a large social network to pay 430 million euros. Why? Because the company moved the personal data of European users onto servers in the United States, and EU law says that is only allowed when the data stays well protected. Officials checked, and they decided it was not. So the company has six months to stop …

🗣 开口说

  1. Do you think a government should be able to stop a company from moving data to another country? Why or why not?
  2. Would you give up some privacy if it meant free apps and faster services? Explain with an example from your own life.
  3. If your country fined a foreign technology company, what should that money be used for?

角色扮演：The press conference

  • 场景：A social network has just been fined 430 million euros. At a press conference, journalists ask why the company keeps appealing while users lose control of their own data.
  • 角色：Journalist: ask hard questions about safety, the deadline and who really pays the price.
  • 角色：Company spokesperson: defend the business, promise fixes, and avoid admitting fault.

✍️ 写作练习

题目：Some people believe that governments should fine technology companies for mishandling personal data, while others think such penalties damage innovation. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Personal data is now one of the most valuable commodities of the internet age, and Europe's fine on a social network asks who should pay when it is mishandled. In my view, financial penalties are necessary, provided they are fair and clearly defined. Supporters of heavy fines argue that money is the only language global companies understand. A technology firm rarely changes its behaviour after a polite warning, but a penalty worth several months of profit concentrates the minds of executives immediately …

📝 小练习（2 题）

1. Why did EU regulators fine the social network?

  • A. It charged European users hidden subscription fees.
  • B. It moved European users' personal data to US servers without adequate protection.
  • C. It refused to delete the accounts of underage users.
  • D. It published false information during an election.

2. What must the company do within six months?

  • A. Stop the data transfers.
  • B. Pay the fine in two instalments.
  • C. Move its European headquarters to Brussels.
  • D. Sell the network to a European investor.
✅ 参考答案与解析（点击展开）

答案速查：1.B　2.A
