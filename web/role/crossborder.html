📖 正文 · CEFR A2

EU fines a social network 430 million euros

Regulators in the European Union have fined a large social network 430 million euros. The network sent personal data about European users to servers in the United States. EU law says that this data must be safe, and the regulators said it was not safe enough. The company has about thirty million users in Europe. It must stop the transfers in six months. The company does not agree and wants to appeal, so the fine may change.

📚 核心词汇（10 个）

#单词词性中文英文释义例句
1regulatorn.监管机构；监管者An official body or person that makes sure companies obey the rules of an industry.The Irish regulator opened a new inquiry into how the app handles children's data.
2finev.罚款To make a person or company pay money as a punishment for breaking a rule.Authorities fined the operator after discovering that safety checks had been skipped.
3transfern.传输；转移（数据）The movement of something from one place, system or country to another.Every international money transfer is recorded and checked for fraud.
4personal data ⭐n. phr.个人数据；个人信息Information about a person that can identify them, such as a name, address or phone number.Giving your date of birth means handing over personal data to a stranger.
5safeguardn.保障措施A measure designed to prevent harm or misuse.Encryption is a basic safeguard for any company that stores customer records.
6violatev.违反；侵犯To break a law, rule or agreement.The court found that the site had violated users' privacy rights.
7rulingn.裁决；判决An official decision made by a court or a body with authority.The ruling surprised many analysts, who had expected a much smaller penalty.
8appeal ⭐v.上诉；申诉To ask a higher court or authority to examine and change a decision.The firm said it will appeal the decision within the next three months.
9compliancen.合规；遵从The act of doing what a rule, law or standard requires.The bank hired dozens of staff to deal with compliance paperwork.
10penaltyn.处罚；罚金A punishment, usually a payment of money, for breaking a rule.Repeat offenders face a penalty that can reach four percent of global revenue.

角色扮演：The press conference

  • 场景：A social network has just been fined 430 million euros. At a press conference, journalists ask why the company keeps appealing while users lose control of their own data.
  • 角色：Journalist: ask hard questions about safety, the deadline and who really pays the price.
  • 角色：Company spokesperson: defend the business, promise fixes, and avoid admitting fault.

2. What must the company do within six months?

  • A. Stop the data transfers.
  • B. Pay the fine in two instalments.
  • C. Move its European headquarters to Brussels.
  • D. Sell the network to a European investor.

  • A. attract public attention
  • B. ask a higher authority to review the decision
  • C. reach a settlement quietly
  • D. cancel the company's licence
