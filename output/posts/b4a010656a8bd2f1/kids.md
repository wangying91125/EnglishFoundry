## 📖 正文 · CEFR A1

**Europe Says the Big App Must Stop**

Europe says a big social app did something wrong. The app took people's info and sent it to the United States. Europe says that is not OK. The app must pay a lot of money. It must stop sending the info away. The app says it will fight the rule. Many people in Europe use the app every day.
