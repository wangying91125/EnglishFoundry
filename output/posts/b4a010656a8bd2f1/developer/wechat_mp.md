## 一、事件原文（B1）

European Union regulators have imposed a 430 million euro penalty on a major social network after finding that it moved personal data belonging to European users onto servers in the United States without adequate legal safeguards. Data protection law in the bloc permits cross border transfers only when the receiving country offers protection that is essentially equivalent to EU standards, a test the network failed to meet. Because roughly thirty million people in Europe use the service, the ruling has consequences far beyond the single company concerned. Authorities have given the firm a six month deadline to suspend the transfers, although it disputes the findings and intends to appeal, which means the sum could still be reduced.
