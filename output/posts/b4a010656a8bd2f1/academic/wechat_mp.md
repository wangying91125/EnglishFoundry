Transfers of that kind are lawful only where the destination jurisdiction delivers a level of protection essentially equivalent to the Union's own, and the regulator concluded that the company's contractual assurances were insufficient to bridge that gap. With some thirty million European account holders, the decision signals how far the bloc is prepared to push its digital rules beyond its borders. The company has been ordered to suspend the flows within six months; it contests the findings and has signalled an appeal, leaving both the amount and the timetable open to revision.
