## 📖 正文 · CEFR A2

**EU fines a social network 430 million euros**

Regulators in the European Union have fined a large social network 430 million euros. The network sent personal data about European users to servers in the United States. EU law says that this data must be safe, and the regulators said it was not safe enough. The company has about thirty million users in Europe. It must stop the transfers in six months. The company does not agree and wants to appeal, so the fine may change.
