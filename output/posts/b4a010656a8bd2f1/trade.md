## 📖 正文 · CEFR B1

**European regulators fine a social network 430 million euros over data transfers**

Regulators in the European Union have fined a large social network 430 million euros. The company sent personal data of European users to servers in the United States. Under EU law, such transfers need strong legal protection, and officials found that protection was missing. The network serves about thirty million users in Europe, so the case affects many people. Officials ordered the company to stop the transfers within six months. A company spokesperson said it disagrees with the ruling and will appeal, so the final penalty may still change.

## 🔎 逐句精读

- Regulators in the European Union have fined a large social network 430 million euros.

- The company sent personal data of European users to servers in the United States.
  - 译：该公司把欧洲用户的个人数据传输到了设在美国的服务器上。
- Under EU law, such transfers need strong legal protection, and officials found that protection was missing.
  - 译：根据欧盟法律，这类数据传输需要强有力的法律保护，而监管方认定相关保护并不存在。
- The network serves about thirty million users in Europe, so the case affects many people.
  - 译：该社交网络在欧洲服务约三千万用户，因此本案影响面很广。
- Officials ordered the company to stop the transfers within six months.
  - 译：监管方责令该公司在六个月内停止这些数据传输。
